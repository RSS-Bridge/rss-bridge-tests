Nvidia Built a MacBook Pro for Windows. Here's the Catch


For twenty years, the answer for serious work was "just buy a MacBook". Microsoft and Nvidia just built a Windows laptop to end that: the Surface Laptop Ultra, with Nvidia's RTX Spark chip. Is Windows finally better? We check it spec for spec.

The trick isn't the processor, it's the memory. A normal laptop's graphics card has 8 or 16 GB of its own; RTX Spark puts a processor with up to 20 cores, Nvidia graphics with up to 6,144 CUDA cores and up to 128 GB of shared memory on one chip, the way Apple's M chips work. So big AI models run on the laptop itself, offline: in Hardware Canucks' test, a mid-sized model ran more than 5× faster than on an RTX 5070 gaming laptop, simply because it fit. But Apple's M5 Max was still faster on most of their tests. Games: +65% over the M5 Pro in Cyberpunk, but anti-cheat games like Valorant, Call of Duty and Battlefield didn't run. Price: from $2,599.99 (24 GB, 512 GB) against $2,499 for a 14-inch MacBook Pro M5 Pro with the same memory and double the storage; only the 128 GB top model is cheaper than the Mac ($5,899.99 vs $6,699, with half the storage).

2FA już NIE CHRONI Twojego konta? 🚨 Prawda o kradzieży ciasteczek!


Uważasz, że silne hasło i weryfikacja dwuetapowa (2FA / SMS / Authenticator) czynią Twoje konto nietykalnym? 🤯 Niestety, to mit. Hakerzy znaleźli metodę, która całkowicie omija fazę logowania – kradną aktywne tokeny sesyjne (pliki cookie) i wchodzą na Twoje konto bankowe, e-mail czy social media bez podawania jakichkolwiek danych!
